# 艺术家风格指南 — 20+ 传奇海报设计师

从 19 世纪先驱到当代大师，涵盖各时期最具影响力的海报设计师视觉语言。

## 按时期速查

**Belle Époque 先驱 (1870s-1900s)**
Jules Chéret, Toulouse-Lautrec, Alphonse Mucha, Steinlen, Eugène Grasset

**现代主义大师 (1920s-1960s)**
A.M. Cassandre, Saul Bass, Josef Müller-Brockmann, Paul Rand, Milton Glaser

**电影海报传奇 (1970s-1990s)**
Drew Struzan

**Mondo 时代 (2000s-至今)**
Olly Moss, Tyler Stout, Martin Ansin, Laurent Durieux, Jay Ryan, Kilian Eng

**当代创新者**
Shepard Fairey, Dan McCarthy, Jock, Paula Scher

## Belle Époque 先驱

### Jules Chéret (1836-1932) — "现代海报之父"

- 明亮欢快的色彩，理想化的女性形象（"Chérettes"），动态运动
- **提示词修饰：** `Jules Chéret style, Belle Époque poster, bright joyful colors, idealized feminine figure in motion, dynamic energy, hand-drawn typography, vintage 1890s Paris poster art`
- **适合：** 庆典、演出、娱乐、浪漫喜剧

### Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec (1864-1901) — "蒙马特大师"

- 平涂色块，日本木刻影响（浮世绘），简化形态，大胆剪影，限 3-5 色
- **提示词修饰：** `Toulouse-Lautrec style Mondo poster, flat color blocks, Japanese print influence, simplified bold silhouettes, 3-color lithograph: black, red, yellow, Montmartre cabaret aesthetic`
- **适合：** 夜生活、音乐场所、戏剧表演、波希米亚主题

### Alphonse Mucha (1860-1939) — "Art Nouveau 图标"

- 华丽花卉图案，流畅有机线条（鞭梢曲线），拜占庭/中世纪装饰
- **提示词修饰：** `Alphonse Mucha Art Nouveau style, flowing organic curves, ornate floral patterns, idealized feminine figure, decorative byzantine border, pastel harmonies: soft pink, sage green, gold`
- **适合：** 时代剧、浪漫电影、奇幻、优雅设计

### Théophile Steinlen (1859-1923) — "Le Chat Noir 大师"

- 社会现实主义，表现力线条，强对比，猫主题，工人阶级题材
- **提示词修饰：** `Steinlen style poster, social realist approach, expressive bold line work, high contrast, cat motif, 3-color print: black, red, cream, Le Chat Noir aesthetic`
- **适合：** 都市剧、社会评论、黑色电影

### Eugène Grasset (1845-1917) — "装饰艺术先驱"

- 中世纪/哥特影响，彩色玻璃美学，粗轮廓形态，对称构图
- **提示词修饰：** `Eugène Grasset style, medieval Gothic influence, stained glass aesthetic, bold outlined forms, symmetrical decorative composition, vintage Art Nouveau poster`
- **适合：** 奇幻、历史史诗、宗教主题

## 现代主义大师

### A.M. Cassandre (1901-1968) — "现代主义几何大师"

- 立体主义/未来主义影响，戏剧性透视，平面几何，大胆无衬线
- **提示词修饰：** `Cassandre style Mondo poster, Cubist geometric planes, dramatic forced perspective, monumental scale, bold Art Deco typography, 3-color print: navy, red, cream, 1930s travel poster aesthetic`
- **适合：** 旅行、冒险、建筑电影、Art Deco 场景

### Saul Bass (1920-1996) — "极简电影海报之王"

- 激进简化，象征性抽象，大胆几何形状，限 2-4 色，负空间大师
- **提示词修饰：** `Saul Bass style Mondo poster, radical minimalist abstraction, bold geometric shapes, symbolic visual metaphor, 3-color screen print: burnt orange, black, cream, negative space mastery, kinetic energy`
- **适合：** 惊悚片、心理剧、黑色电影、任何需要概念方法的电影

### Josef Müller-Brockmann (1914-1996) — "瑞士国际风格大师"

- 网格布局，数学精度，Helvetica，客观/中性，主色，干净留白
- **提示词修饰：** `Josef Müller-Brockmann Swiss style, mathematical grid layout, Helvetica typography, objective minimal design, primary colors: red, blue, yellow, black, 1960s rationalist design`
- **适合：** 纪录片、建筑电影、技术主题

### Paul Rand (1914-1996) — "企业现代主义先驱"

- 俏皮几何，巧妙视觉双关，大胆简单形态，限色，智慧与机智
- **提示词修饰：** `Paul Rand style poster, playful geometric forms, clever visual wordplay, bold simple shapes, limited palette: 2-3 colors, witty intelligent design, 1950s American design`
- **适合：** 企业主题、智性喜剧、设计聚焦主题

### Milton Glaser (1929-2020) — "波普艺术字体创新者"

- 迷幻色彩组合，Art Nouveau 复兴，创新排版，超现实元素
- **提示词修饰：** `Milton Glaser style poster, psychedelic color palette, Art Nouveau flowing lines, innovative typographic design, vibrant joyful colors, 1960s pop culture aesthetic`
- **适合：** 音乐、反文化主题、传记电影、庆典

## 电影海报传奇

### Drew Struzan (1947-) — "好莱坞大片传奇"

- 绘画级照片写实，史诗级电影构图，温暖怀旧光效，角色聚焦
- **提示词修饰：** `Drew Struzan style Mondo poster, painted realism adapted for screen print, epic cinematic composition, character portraits integrated, warm nostalgic lighting glow, 1980s adventure poster aesthetic`
- **适合：** 冒险、大片、系列电影、群像

## Mondo 时代

### Olly Moss (1987-) — "当代极简主义大师"

- 超极简构图，巧妙负空间，视觉双重含义，1-3 色，图标物体优于面孔
- **提示词修饰：** `Olly Moss style Mondo poster, ultra minimalist design, clever negative space, hidden imagery within silhouette, 2-color limited palette, geometric simplicity, visual double meaning`
- **适合：** 有标志性意象的电影、概念主题、黑色电影

### Tyler Stout (1976-) — "极大化细节之王"

- 密集角色拼贴，精细线条，极大化构图，漫画影响，每一寸都利用
- **提示词修饰：** `Tyler Stout style Mondo poster, densely layered composition, character collage, intricate line work, high contrast black ink lines, organized maximalist design, comic book influenced`
- **适合：** 群像电影、系列、史诗故事

### Martin Ansin (1977-) — "Art Deco 优雅"

- Art Deco 影响，优雅线条，柔和复古色板，精致构图，建筑元素
- **提示词修饰：** `Martin Ansin style Mondo poster, Art Deco influence, elegant flowing lines, muted vintage palette, sophisticated composition, architectural elements, refined line work`
- **适合：** 经典好莱坞、时代剧、哥特恐怖

### Laurent Durieux (1968-) — "视觉双关大师"

- 巧妙隐藏图像，氛围光效，象征性构图，微妙渐变，神秘氛围
- **提示词修饰：** `Laurent Durieux style Mondo poster, visual pun composition, hidden symbolic imagery, atmospheric lighting, subtle gradient within screen print style, mysterious evocative mood`
- **适合：** 惊悚片、悬疑片、有反转的电影

### Jay Ryan (1971-) — "民间手工魅力"

- 单一焦点图像，大胆简单形状，纹理背景，温暖大地色调，手工感
- **提示词修饰：** `Jay Ryan style Mondo poster, single focal image, bold simple shapes, textured hand-printed background, folksy handmade aesthetic, warm limited palette`
- **适合：** 成长题材、独立剧、怀旧故事

### Kilian Eng (1983-) — "几何未来主义"

- 精确技术线条，几何科幻建筑，冷色板，等距透视，数字精度，霓虹点缀
- **提示词修饰：** `Kilian Eng style Mondo poster, geometric futuristic design, precise technical line work, sci-fi architectural elements, cool teal and purple palette, angular isometric perspective, atmospheric neon-lit depth`
- **适合：** 科幻、赛博朋克、未来场景

## 当代创新者

### Shepard Fairey (1970-) — "街头艺术革命者"

- 大胆宣传风格，红黑奶油配色，半调人像，政治信息，模板美学
- **提示词修饰：** `Shepard Fairey style poster, bold propaganda aesthetic, red black cream palette, halftone portrait, political street art influence, stencil layers, OBEY aesthetic`
- **适合：** 政治电影、传记剧、社会正义主题

### Dan McCarthy (1973-) — "几何抽象"

- 超平面色面，几何景观抽象，2-4 色限色，无渐变，建筑简化
- **提示词修饰：** `Dan McCarthy style Mondo poster, ultra-flat geometric abstraction, simplified landscape forms, 2-3 solid color planes, no gradients, architectural nature shapes, stark minimalist contrast`
- **适合：** 西部片、公路片、极简叙事

### Jock (1972-) — "粗犷动态能量"

- 粗糙表现力笔触，动态动作姿势，高对比光效，粗犷纹理，漫画强度
- **提示词修饰：** `Jock style Mondo poster, rough expressive brushwork, dynamic action composition, high contrast chiaroscuro lighting, gritty textured surfaces, comic book intensity, raw kinetic energy`
- **适合：** 动作片、惊悚片、犯罪剧、恐怖

### Paula Scher (1948-) — "字体极大化"

- 字体作为主要视觉，分层文字构图，鲜艳色彩，信息密度，表现性字型
- **提示词修饰：** `Paula Scher style poster, typography as primary visual element, layered text composition, vibrant color palette, dense information design, expressive hand-drawn letterforms`
- **适合：** 音乐活动、音乐节、文字密集主题

## 混合艺术家风格

```
[电影] in Mondo poster style, [艺术家 A] 的 [特征] 遇上 [艺术家 B] 的 [特征],
[共同视觉元素], [组合配色方法]
```

**示例组合：**
- Saul Bass 极简 + Toulouse-Lautrec 平涂 → 黑色惊悚
- Olly Moss 负空间 + Cassandre 几何 → 科幻史诗
- Mucha Art Nouveau + Kilian Eng 未来主义 → 奇幻赛博朋克
- Jock 粗犷 + Tyler Stout 细节 → 动作群像
